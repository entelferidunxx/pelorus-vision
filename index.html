The Industry Problem

The operational gap between the deck and the claim

Laytime is decided by minutes, yet the record of those minutes is still assembled by hand under time pressure.

1

Handwritten paper logbooks

Entries are transcribed after the fact, are easily illegible or incomplete, and can't be independently verified.

2

Subjective crew timing

Officers on watch juggle cargo, safety and paperwork. Start and stop times differ between ship, terminal and agent.

3

Multi-million-dollar claims

Demurrage and despatch disputes hinge on contested timelines, often settled months later with weak evidence.

4

Strenuous PSC & vetting scrutiny

Port State Control and vetting inspections demand consistent, traceable records of cargo and deck operations.

5

Weather stoppage ambiguity

Whether rain genuinely halted work, and for how long, is the most contested line in many Statements of Facts.

Minutes = $$$

Every unrecorded or disputed minute carries direct financial exposure for owners, charterers and operators.

System Architecture

Edge perception, cloud-grade reasoning

Inference runs on the vessel so it keeps working with intermittent connectivity. Only structured events and evidence sync ashore.

Industrial deck cameras

Marine-grade, IP-rated units covering cranes, hatches and manifolds day and night.

Edge AI node

Onboard GPU inference detects and classifies events in real time with no video streaming required.

Multimodal reasoning

Vision-language models verify context, resolve ambiguity and draft human-readable log entries.

Tamper-evident ledger

Events are hashed, signed and synced to produce audit-proof SOFs and digital logs.

Designed for the deck

  • Offline-first operation with store-and-forward sync
  • Low-bandwidth: events and key frames, not continuous video
  • Rugged hardware for salt, vibration and temperature extremes

Built for trust

  • Cryptographic hashing and signing of every record
  • Evidence frame linked to each timeline entry
  • Human review and override with full audit trail
